# Functional Example

A Functional artifact should provide enough evidence to support
`Available + Functional`, while still falling short of `Reusable`.

## Typical Evidence Pattern

- the artifact has a clear entry document;
- the reviewer can reconstruct the environment with bounded effort;
- a documented representative workflow runs and produces fresh output; and
- the output can be mapped back to a paper claim.

## Why This Does Not Automatically Imply Reusable

Functional evidence shows that the released workflow works for the intended
review route. It does not by itself show that the artifact supports transfer to
another input, another bounded use case, or another reviewer-defined task.

## Common Boundary

A case often stops at Functional when the artifact exposes only the original
paper workflow, but does not provide a reviewer-checkable transfer task, reuse
instruction, or bounded secondary validation route.
